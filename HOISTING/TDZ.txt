The Temporal Dead Zone is the period of time between the creation of a let or const variable and its actual initialization.
 Because let and const are hoisted to the top of their block but are not given an undefined placeholder like var, 
accessing them before their line of code is executed results in a ReferenceError. 
The TDZ exists to catch errors early and prevent bugs caused by using variables before they hold actual data

Temporal means "related to time," not space or lines of code.

In JavaScript, the TDZ is the period of time between when a let or const variable is hoisted (put into memory) and when it is actually initialized (assigned a real value).
